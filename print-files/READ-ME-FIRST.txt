SUN SHIELD - PRINT FILES

Filament: PETG (dry it first). Print everything in the same filament and settings.

Each step has ready-to-print PLATE files (.3mf). Open one in Bambu Studio and every part
is already on the bed, standing the right way and spaced out. Just add the brim and
support settings below and slice.
  - Don't use Auto Orient or Arrange: both undo the orientation the parts were designed for.
  - Don't turn on fuzzy skin: it roughens the walls enough to ruin the joint fits.
The single-part STLs are in each "individual-parts" folder, only if you need one again.


STEP 1: TEST PRINTS
Folder: step-1_test-prints
  Open test-plate.3mf (4 small pieces). Add a 5 mm brim (they're tall and thin). No supports.

  It has one "fixed" piece and three "fit" pieces. Try each fit piece in the fixed one.
  The notches on each fit piece mark its size: 1 = 0.10, 2 = 0.15, 3 = 0.20.
  Pick the TIGHTEST one that slides together by hand with no visible gap at the front.


STEP 2: THE SHIELD
Folder: step-2_full-shield
  Open ONLY the folder matching the size you picked in step 1:
      0.10mm_1-notch    0.15mm_2-notch    0.20mm_3-notch

  Print its four plates:
      plate-A_outer-and-middle.3mf         4 parts   brim 10 mm
      plate-B_inner-and-webcam-shelf.3mf   3 parts   brim 10 mm
      plate-C_left-side-extension.3mf      1 part    no brim (it fills the bed)
      plate-D_right-side-extension.3mf     1 part    no brim
  Brim on A and B: those parts stand on the tips of their joints, so they need it.

  Supports: only on plates C and D, under the rounded corner where the part
  meets the bed. Paint them there, or use tree supports set to "on build plate only".
  Before printing, slice plate A once and check it doesn't add supports anywhere else.


SHIPPING TO RYAN
  Don't assemble anything. Ship the pieces separately, packed flat.
  Wrap each piece on its own (bubble wrap or foam): the thin back edge along each
  seam breaks easily.


ALSO NEEDED
  About 200 mm of 1.75 mm filament, as the hinge pin between the two inner pieces.
  One 1/4"-20 screw, 3/8" long, head no wider than 14 mm, for the webcam.
