SUN SHIELD - PRINT FILES

Filament: PETG (dry it first). Print everything in the same filament and settings.


STEP 1: TEST PRINTS (all on one plate)
Folder: step-1_test-prints

  fit-test-clip.stl
      Push it onto a monitor's top edge. It should grip without forcing.
      If it's loose or too tight, stop and tell Ryan.

  fixed_vertical-seam.stl    +  fit_vertical-seam_0.10mm / 0.15mm / 0.20mm
  fixed_horizontal-seam.stl  +  fit_horizontal-seam_0.10mm / 0.15mm / 0.20mm
      The "fixed" piece is the same for all three tests. Try each "fit" piece in it.
      The notches on the edge also mark the size: 1 = 0.10, 2 = 0.15, 3 = 0.20.

  Pick the TIGHTEST one that slides together by hand with no visible gap at the front.
  Use the vertical seam result if the two disagree.


STEP 2: THE SHIELD
Folder: step-2_full-shield

  Open ONLY the folder matching the size you picked in step 1:
      0.10mm_1-notch    0.15mm_2-notch    0.20mm_3-notch

  Each has a left-monitor and a right-monitor folder with 5 parts each.
  Don't mix parts from different size folders.

  Parts, numbered from the outside edge toward the middle:
      1a  side extension, top
      1b  side extension, bottom
      2   outer piece
      3   middle piece (notch for the monitor's built-in camera)
      4   inner piece (left has the webcam shelf; both have the hinge knuckles)

  Lay parts flat, faceted side UP, so support marks end up on the back.
  Keep supports off the dovetail edges, or sand them lightly if they get marks.


ALSO NEEDED
  About 200 mm of 1.75 mm filament, as the hinge pin between the two inner pieces.
  One 1/4"-20 screw, 1/4" to 3/8" long, head no wider than 14 mm, for the webcam.

More detail: PRINTING.md in the main folder.
