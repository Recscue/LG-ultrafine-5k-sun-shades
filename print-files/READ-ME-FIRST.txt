SUN SHIELD - PRINT FILES

Filament: PETG (dry it first). Print everything in the same filament and settings.
Print every file exactly as it opens. Don't use Auto Orient and don't lay parts flat.
Don't turn on fuzzy skin: it roughens the walls enough to ruin the joint fits.


STEP 1: TEST PRINTS (one plate)
Folder: step-1_test-prints

  fixed_seam.stl  +  fit_seam_0.10mm / 0.15mm / 0.20mm
      The "fixed" piece is the same for all three tests. Try each "fit" piece in it.
      The notches on the edge also mark the size: 1 = 0.10, 2 = 0.15, 3 = 0.20.

  Pick the TIGHTEST one that slides together by hand with no visible gap at the front.


STEP 2: THE SHIELD
Folder: step-2_full-shield

  Open ONLY the folder matching the size you picked in step 1:
      0.10mm_1-notch    0.15mm_2-notch    0.20mm_3-notch
  Don't mix parts from different size folders.

  Parts, numbered from the outside edge toward the middle:
      1   side extension (one piece)
      2   outer piece
      3   middle piece (notch for the monitor's built-in camera)
      4   inner piece (has the hinge knuckles)
      5   webcam shelf (left folder only, separate part)

  Plates:
      Plate A: both 2 and both 3 pieces, standing on their seam end (4 parts)
      Plate B: both 4 pieces standing on their seam end, plus the webcam shelf
               flat on its bottom (3 parts)
      Plate C: the left 1 piece, diagonally across the bed, on its flat outer edge
      Plate D: the right 1 piece, same way
  Brim: 10 mm on plates A and B (those parts stand on the tips of their joints).
        No brim on C and D (the diagonal fills the bed, and the flat edge holds it).
  Supports: only under the rounded corners of the 1 pieces, where they meet the bed.


SHIPPING TO RYAN
  Don't assemble anything. Ship the pieces separate, packed flat.
  Wrap each piece on its own (bubble wrap or foam): the thin back edge along each
  seam breaks easily.


ALSO NEEDED
  About 200 mm of 1.75 mm filament, as the hinge pin between the two inner pieces.
  One 1/4"-20 screw, 3/8" long, head no wider than 14 mm, for the webcam.
